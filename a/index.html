01 — The main thing

Weekly meal prep,
done at restaurant standard.

Most meal prep is a tray of chicken and rice that tastes worse every day it sits. This is a menu built for your household, cooked the day it is delivered, and engineered so day four eats like day one.

Groceries sourced and included. No contract — skip any week by the Wednesday before. Pricing shown is a starting point; the quote depends on household size, dietary tracks and protein.

Why it holds up

The tenth plate looks
like the first.

That is the entire skill of a service line, and it is the reason restaurant cooks are good at meal prep. Portioning, holding, cooling, what to sauce and what to keep dry, which vegetable survives Thursday and which one has to be eaten Monday — that is not guesswork, it is the job.

Fifteen years of doing it three hundred covers at a time is what you are actually buying when you buy a week of food.

  • Cooked the day it is delivered — never batch-cooked days ahead
  • Sauces and crisp elements packed separately where they need to be
  • Every container labelled with the dish and how to bring it back up

    05 — Who is cooking

    Rashard Smiley.

    Fifteen years in professional kitchens, most of it in South Florida, most of it at volume. I came up on lines where the standard was set by somebody harder to please than any client I have cooked for since.

    I spent three years at BLT Prime inside Trump National Doral — a AAA Four Diamond resort, heavy covers, no room to be sloppy. I ran back-of-house as head chef in Boca Raton for five years, trained the teams, and opened new stores. Before all of that I was the lead pitmaster on a family mobile kitchen doing large-format events across South Florida, which is where I learned to cook for two hundred people without the food knowing the difference.

    Now I cook for households in Palm Beach County. Same standard. Smaller room.
